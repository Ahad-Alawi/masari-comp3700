Skill Learning Calculator

Estimate how many weeks it may take you to finish a short learning plan.

This is a planning estimate, not a guarantee of learning or certification. You decide the study-hour target.
Please enter a valid target learning hours.
Please enter a valid hours per week.

How the estimate works

Adjusted hours = target hours × experience factor × practice factor.

Weeks = adjusted hours ÷ hours available per week, rounded up. The experience factor is 1 for beginners and 0.8 for students with some experience. The practice factor is 1.2 if you select extra practice.
