Your Future Starts Here

Many students in Oman find it hard to choose a university major because they do not know much about the jobs available after graduation. I made Masari to help students explore majors, compare them, and learn about possible careers.

How Masari works

  1. Browse the list of majors and read short descriptions.
  2. Compare the majors you like in the comparison table.
  3. Check the employment trends and the skills employers ask for.
  4. Register to save majors and ask an academic advisor a question.

Key terms

Major
The main subject a student studies at university.
Employment trend
How the number of employed graduates changes over the years.
Skill
An ability, such as programming or communication, that employers value.
