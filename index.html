Welcome to Masari

Find your path. Choose your major with facts.

Many students in Oman find it hard to choose a university major because they do not know much about the jobs available after graduation. I made Masari to help students explore majors, compare them, and learn about possible careers.

About Masari

Masari is a student website project for COMP3700. It helps students in Oman learn about majors and possible career paths. Employment numbers are examples, not official statistics.
