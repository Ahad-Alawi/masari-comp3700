About Masari

A student project to make information about majors and careers easier to explore.

Masari (Arabic for “my path”) is a website I made for COMP3700 at Sultan Qaboos University. The idea came from how difficult it can be for students to choose a major without knowing much about career opportunities in Oman.

My goals

  • Help students explore majors in one place.
  • Show examples of useful skills and career options.
  • Make employment information easier to understand.

Data sources

Employment figures in this project are samples, not official Oman statistics. A future version could use published statistics from the National Centre for Statistics and Information (NCSI).
