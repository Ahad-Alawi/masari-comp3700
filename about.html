About Masari

Masari (in Arabic, “my path”) is a website I made for the Introduction to Web Computing course (COMP3700) at Sultan Qaboos University. The idea came from how difficult it can be for students to choose a major without knowing much about future job opportunities in Oman.

At this stage some features are only examples. The forms send their data to a test website, and the numbers in the tables are sample data. I plan to improve both in the next parts of the project.

Our goals

  • Give students one place to explore majors.
  • Make employment information easy to understand.
  • Connect students with academic advisors.

Data sources

Employment information in the final website will come from official published statistics, such as the National Centre for Statistics and Information (NCSI) of Oman. Every chart will show its source and year. The figures in this prototype are samples.
